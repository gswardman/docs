# Get Your Charity Found by AI

When someone asks ChatGPT, Google, Perplexity or Claude which charity to support, the answer only names charities that AI can find and trust. This page is the short checklist. The full guide, with the data behind each step, is [How to Get Your Charity Found by AI](https://www.giveready.org/learn/get-your-charity-found-by-ai).

## The checklist

1. **Check what AI says about you today.** Ask four AI tools five questions: a donor question, a question the people you help would ask, a funder question, "is [name] a legitimate charity?" and "what does [name] do?". Note whether each names you.
2. **Make your facts identical everywhere.** Same name, registration number, address and one-sentence description on your regulator's register, your website, Candid or Charity Navigator, Google, LinkedIn and GiveReady.
3. **Let AI read your website.** Check that robots.txt does not block AI crawlers, open your About page with who you are and what you do, and add structured data with your registration number.
4. **Be listed where AI already reads.** Registers, directories, partner and funder pages, local news, and your [GiveReady profile](https://www.giveready.org/claim).
5. **Check again monthly.** Changes show up over weeks, not days.
